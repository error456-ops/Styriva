Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how STYRIVA handles personal information, uploaded images and information submitted when you use our AI styling platform.

Last updated: November 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to STYRIVA ("STYRIVA", "we", "us", or "our"). STYRIVA is an AI-powered fashion and styling platform that helps users explore outfits and receive styling recommendations.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected or processed when you use STYRIVA, why we use it, when it may be shared and what choices may be available to you.

Some STYRIVA features may require you to create an account or sign in. Other features may be available without an account.

This policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions and Cookie Policy.

2. Information We Collect or Process

The information involved depends on which STYRIVA features you use and what you choose to submit.

2.1 Account Information

If you create an account, we may collect and process information such as:

  • Name or username, if provided.
  • Email address.
  • Account credentials and authentication information.
  • Account settings and preferences.
  • Account status and relevant activity records.

Passwords should be stored using appropriate password-protection methods rather than as readable plain text.

2.2 Images You Upload

STYRIVA allows users to submit images for styling and related features. These may include:

  • Images of clothing and garments.
  • Images of tops, bottoms and dresses.
  • Images of shoes, scarves and accessories.
  • Reference or inspiration photographs.
  • User or model photographs, where submitted.
  • Other images voluntarily provided for a feature.

2.3 Styling Information

When you make a styling request, we may process information you provide, such as:

  • Occasion or event.
  • Style, aesthetic or vibe preferences.
  • Weather information.
  • Garment type or clothing preferences.
  • Style Evolution preferences.
  • Other selections or instructions submitted with a request.

2.4 Usage, Credits and Transactions

We may process information needed to operate and manage usage of STYRIVA, including:

  • Generation and feature usage.
  • Free usage allowances and limits.
  • Credit balances and credit consumption.
  • Generation status and error records.
  • Payment or transaction references, where paid features are offered.

2.5 Technical and Device Information

When you visit STYRIVA, our website and infrastructure may process technical information, including:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device type and operating system.
  • Pages or features accessed.
  • Approximate access times.
  • Server logs and security events.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information processed through STYRIVA for purposes such as:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Providing AI-powered styling features.
  • Processing uploaded clothing and reference images.
  • Generating outfit recommendations and visual results.
  • Providing Style Evolution and related features.
  • Managing credits, usage allowances and feature limits.
  • Processing and investigating payment-related issues where applicable.
  • Maintaining platform security and preventing abuse.
  • Diagnosing errors and improving reliability.
  • Responding to support requests and complaints.
  • Meeting legal and regulatory obligations.

We aim to use information only for purposes that are relevant to providing, maintaining and protecting the service or otherwise permitted by applicable law.

4. AI and Image Processing

STYRIVA uses artificial intelligence to process information and images submitted through its styling features.

Depending on the feature, your uploaded images, prompts, garment information and styling instructions may be sent to third-party technology providers to generate the requested result.

AI Processing Provider

STYRIVA uses OpenAI services for certain AI-related processing. Information submitted to an AI feature may be transmitted to OpenAI as necessary to provide that feature.

Image Storage and Processing Provider

STYRIVA uses Cloudinary for image-related services. Depending on the feature and configuration, images may be uploaded to, stored, delivered or processed through Cloudinary.

The processing of information by these providers is also subject to their applicable terms, privacy documentation and the arrangements governing our use of their services.

Please upload only images you have the right to use. Avoid submitting sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary for the feature you are using.

5. Uploaded Images and Generated Results

Images submitted to STYRIVA are processed to provide the styling feature requested by the user.

Generated results may also be processed or stored so that the relevant feature can function.

We may retain uploaded images, generated results and related records for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the requested feature, maintaining the service, preventing abuse and meeting legal obligations.

Actual retention periods may differ by information type, feature, storage provider and technical configuration.

We do not promise that every uploaded image or generated result is automatically deleted immediately after a generation is completed.

If you want information deleted, you may contact us using the details in this policy. We will review the request under applicable law and our actual retention requirements.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

STYRIVA may use cookies, browser storage, sessions and similar technologies to support website functionality and security.

Depending on the technologies enabled, these may be used to:

  • Provide essential website functionality.
  • Maintain necessary session information.
  • Remember relevant preferences.
  • Protect against unauthorized activity.
  • Understand website performance and usage, where enabled.
  • Support advertising, where enabled and permitted.

Some cookies or storage technologies may be provided by third-party services.

You can review our Cookie Policy for further information. Browser settings may also allow you to manage or delete certain cookies.

Disabling certain technologies may affect how some parts of STYRIVA function.

7. Advertising and Analytics

STYRIVA may use analytics or advertising services if those services are enabled on the website.

If Google AdSense or another advertising provider is used, that provider may process certain information, such as cookies, device information or IP address, to provide or measure advertisements, subject to applicable settings and law.

Advertising providers may have their own privacy policies and controls.

We will provide appropriate consent mechanisms where required by applicable law before using technologies that require consent.

We do not claim that advertising or analytics services are active unless they have actually been enabled.

8. How We Share Information

We may share or allow access to information where reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure or provide STYRIVA.

Depending on the features used, these parties may include:

  • OpenAI: for AI-related processing requested through STYRIVA.
  • Cloudinary: for image storage, delivery or processing.
  • Railway: for application hosting and related infrastructure.
  • Payment providers: if paid credits or other paid features are offered and a payment provider processes the transaction.
  • Other service providers: where needed for security, communication, technical operations or other functions actually used by STYRIVA.

We may also disclose information where required by law, in response to lawful requests, to protect users or the service, investigate fraud or security incidents, or establish or defend legal claims.

We do not sell personal information as a business practice.

9. International Processing

Some technology providers used by STYRIVA may process information on infrastructure located outside your country.

As a result, information submitted through certain features may be transferred to or processed in other countries, depending on the provider's infrastructure and service configuration.

Where applicable, we will take steps required by relevant data protection laws in relation to such processing and transfers.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information processed through STYRIVA against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Security measures may include access restrictions, authentication controls, infrastructure protections and other safeguards appropriate to the service.

No website, server, database or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We therefore cannot promise absolute security, but we aim to address security risks responsibly.

11. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable law.

Retention may be necessary for:

  • Providing requested STYRIVA features.
  • Maintaining account and service functionality.
  • Managing credits and usage records.
  • Resolving payment disputes, where applicable.
  • Preventing fraud and misuse.
  • Maintaining security and technical logs.
  • Meeting legal or regulatory obligations.

When information is no longer reasonably required, we may delete, anonymize or otherwise dispose of it, subject to applicable legal, technical and operational requirements.

Copies in backups or provider systems may not be deleted immediately and may remain for limited periods under the relevant retention processes.

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • Request information about personal data processed about you.
  • Request access to personal information, where applicable.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally available.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise a grievance or privacy complaint.
  • Exercise other rights provided by applicable law.

These rights may be subject to legal exceptions and verification requirements.

To make a request, contact us using the email address provided in the Contact section below.

If you have a STYRIVA account and want to request its deletion, contact us using the details provided below or use an available account deletion feature if one is provided.

You may also request deletion of personal information or uploaded content where applicable law gives you that right.

We may need to verify your identity or account ownership before acting on a request.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or other legitimate permitted purposes.

17. Contact and Privacy Grievances

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your personal information, uploaded images, account deletion or a privacy concern, please contact us.
