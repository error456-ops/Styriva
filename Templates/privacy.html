Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains how STYRIVA handles information when you use our AI styling platform.

Last updated: August 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to STYRIVA ("STYRIVA", "we", "us", or "our"). STYRIVA is an AI-powered styling platform designed to help users explore fashion and receive personalized styling recommendations.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use STYRIVA, how that information is used, how it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

STYRIVA is currently designed so that users can use the styling service without creating an account or logging in.

By using STYRIVA, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Handle

The information processed by STYRIVA depends on how you use the website and its features.

2.1 No Account Required

STYRIVA does not currently require users to create an account or log in to use the styling service.

We therefore do not currently require account credentials, usernames, or account profiles for ordinary use of the styling features.

2.2 Images You Upload

STYRIVA allows users to upload images for AI styling and related features.

These may include:

  • A user or model photo
  • Images of tops
  • Images of bottoms
  • Images of scarves or accessories
  • Other images voluntarily submitted for styling

2.3 Styling Information

When you create a styling request, STYRIVA may process information you provide such as:

  • Occasion
  • Style or vibe preferences
  • Weather information you provide
  • Style Evolution preferences
  • Other options selected during a styling request

2.4 Usage Information

STYRIVA may process information needed to operate and protect the service, including styling generation usage, Style Evolution usage, daily usage limits, and security events.

Usage limits may be applied to help prevent abuse and manage the availability of AI-powered features.

2.5 Technical Information

When you access STYRIVA, our website and hosting infrastructure may process basic technical information, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Operating system
  • Pages or features accessed
  • Basic server and security logs

This information may be processed by the website, hosting infrastructure, or security systems as necessary to operate and protect STYRIVA.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information processed through STYRIVA to:

  • Provide AI styling services
  • Process uploaded images
  • Generate personalized styling recommendations
  • Provide Style Evolution features
  • Apply usage limits
  • Prevent abuse and unauthorized use
  • Maintain website and platform security
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Maintain and improve STYRIVA
  • Respond to communications submitted by users
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

4. AI and Image Processing

STYRIVA uses artificial intelligence to process information and images submitted by users.

Depending on the feature being used, uploaded images and related styling information may be processed by third-party technology providers that help STYRIVA operate its AI features.

Users should only upload images they have the right to use and should avoid uploading information that they do not want processed by an online service.

5. Uploaded Images and Generated Results

Uploaded images are processed to provide the styling functionality requested by the user.

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights or permissions to upload images to STYRIVA.

Uploaded images and generated results may be stored temporarily or retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, operate the platform, maintain security, prevent abuse, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the feature used, and operational or legal requirements.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

STYRIVA may use cookies, sessions, local storage, and similar technologies when necessary to operate the website and its features.

These technologies may be used to:

  • Provide necessary website functionality
  • Maintain necessary session information
  • Remember necessary settings
  • Protect against abuse
  • Improve website performance
  • Understand website usage where applicable

STYRIVA does not currently use these technologies for user login or account authentication because an account is not required to use the styling service.

Some third-party services used by STYRIVA may also use cookies or similar technologies.

7. Advertising

STYRIVA may display advertisements from third-party advertising providers, including Google AdSense, if advertising is enabled on the website.

If advertising services are enabled, advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize advertisements, subject to applicable law and the settings available to users.

Google and its advertising partners may use advertising cookies to help display and measure relevant advertisements based on a user's visits to STYRIVA and other websites.

Users may be able to manage personalized advertising through Google's advertising controls and other applicable privacy settings.

Where legally required, STYRIVA will provide appropriate consent or privacy controls for cookies and advertising.

8. Information Sharing

STYRIVA may share or provide access to information with service providers that help us operate the platform.

Depending on the services currently used by STYRIVA, these providers may assist with:

  • Website hosting
  • Database and infrastructure services
  • AI processing
  • Image processing
  • Security and abuse prevention
  • Analytics, where enabled
  • Communication services, where used
  • Advertising services, where enabled

We do not sell users' personal information as a business practice.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect users or STYRIVA, investigate security incidents, prevent fraud or abuse, or enforce our policies.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through STYRIVA.

However, no website, server, database, or method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Accordingly, while we work to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

This may include providing STYRIVA, processing styling requests, maintaining platform functionality, preventing abuse, maintaining security, resolving disputes, and complying with legal obligations.

When information is no longer reasonably required, we may delete, anonymize, or otherwise dispose of it, subject to applicable legal, technical, and operational requirements.

11. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain information
  • Ask about how your information is processed
  • Withdraw consent where applicable
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where applicable

To make a privacy-related request, contact STYRIVA using the contact information provided on this website.
