How STYRIVA Works

STYRIVA combines the clothing options you provide with your occasion, preferred vibe and weather to create personalized AI-powered styling recommendations.

From your wardrobe to a curated look

You don't need a completely new wardrobe to create a good outfit. STYRIVA is designed to help you explore the clothing you already own and discover new ways to combine your pieces.

1

Choose your occasion

Tell STYRIVA what kind of situation you're dressing for. Whether it's a casual day, an outing or a special occasion, this information helps guide the styling recommendation.

2

Select your vibe

Choose the visual direction you want your outfit to have. Your selected vibe helps guide the overall styling approach.

3

Add the weather

Provide the relevant weather conditions so practical factors can be considered alongside appearance and personal style.

4

Upload your clothing options

Upload the clothing and accessories you want STYRIVA to consider. Depending on the styling workflow, these may include tops, bottoms, dresses, shoes and scarves.

5

Let STYRIVA analyze your choices

The AI styling process evaluates the images and information you provide to help identify suitable combinations.

6

Receive your styling recommendation

STYRIVA presents a curated styling result based on your selected clothing options and preferences.

What information does STYRIVA use?

Your styling recommendation is based on the clothing images, preferences and other information you provide during the styling process.

Clothing

The clothing pieces you upload form the starting point for creating outfit combinations. This can include tops, bottoms and dresses.

Accessories

Available accessories, such as shoes and scarves, can be considered as part of the overall styling combination.

Occasion

Your intended occasion helps guide whether a clothing combination is appropriate for the situation.

Vibe

Your selected vibe gives the recommendation a particular visual direction and styling mood.

Weather

Weather information can influence the practicality and suitability of the recommended combination.

Your provided images

Uploaded images allow the styling system to work with the specific clothing pieces and other visual options you want to use.

How AI helps with the recommendation

STYRIVA uses artificial intelligence as part of its styling process to help evaluate available clothing choices and produce a recommendation.

From multiple choices to one curated direction

When several clothing options are available, there can be many possible combinations. STYRIVA's styling process is designed to evaluate those possibilities against factors such as the occasion, vibe, weather and visual harmony.

The goal is to help narrow those choices into a practical, personalized styling direction rather than simply listing every possible combination.

AI-generated recommendations are suggestions. Personal preference, comfort, fit and real-world circumstances should always be considered when deciding what to wear.

What happens after you generate a look?

Once the styling process is complete, STYRIVA takes you to your result so you can review the recommended combination and accompanying styling guidance.

Curated combination

Review the clothing and accessory combination selected from the options you provided.

Styling guidance

Explore the styling suggestions and practical guidance associated with your recommended look.

Your inspiration

Use the recommendation as inspiration and adjust it according to your personal taste and comfort.

AI results are not guaranteed to be perfect. Image interpretation and generated recommendations may occasionally contain inaccuracies.
