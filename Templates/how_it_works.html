How STYRIVA Works

STYRIVA combines the clothing options you provide with your occasion, preferred vibe, and weather to create a personalized styling recommendation.

From your wardrobe to a curated look

You don't need a completely new wardrobe to create a good outfit. STYRIVA is designed to work with the options you already have and help you decide how they can work together.

1

Choose your occasion

Start by telling STYRIVA what kind of situation you're dressing for. The occasion provides context for the styling recommendation.

2

Select your vibe

Choose the visual direction you want your outfit to have. Your selected vibe helps guide the overall styling approach.

3

Add the weather

Provide the relevant weather conditions so that practical factors can be considered alongside appearance.

4

Upload your options

Provide the clothing and accessory choices you want STYRIVA to consider when creating the look.

5

Let STYRIVA analyze the choices

The styling system evaluates the available combinations against the information you've provided.

6

Receive your recommendation

STYRIVA presents a curated styling result designed around your selected options and preferences.

What information does STYRIVA use?

The recommendation is based on the information and options you provide during the styling process.

Clothing

The clothing pieces you make available to STYRIVA form the starting point for the outfit combinations.

Accessories

Available accessories can be considered as part of the overall styling combination.

Occasion

The intended occasion helps the system determine whether a combination suits the context.

Vibe

Your selected vibe gives the recommendation a particular visual direction.

Weather

Weather conditions can influence the practicality and suitability of the recommended combination.

Your provided images

Images you upload allow STYRIVA's styling system to work with the specific items you want to use.

How AI helps with the recommendation

STYRIVA uses AI as part of its styling process to help evaluate the available choices and produce a recommendation.

From multiple choices to one curated direction

When several clothing options are available, there can be many possible combinations. STYRIVA's styling process is designed to evaluate those possibilities against factors such as the occasion, vibe, weather, and visual harmony.

The goal is to narrow those choices into a practical, personalized styling direction rather than simply listing every possible combination.

AI-generated recommendations are suggestions. Personal preference, comfort, fit, and real-world circumstances should always be considered when deciding what to wear.

What happens after you generate a look?

Once the styling process is complete, STYRIVA takes you to your result where you can review the recommended combination and the accompanying styling guidance.

Curated combination

See the clothing and accessory combination selected from the options you provided.

Styling guidance

Review the reasoning and practical suggestions associated with the recommended look.

A starting point

Use the recommendation as inspiration and adjust it according to your own personal taste and comfort.
