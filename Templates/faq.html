Frequently Asked Questions

Questions, answered.

Find answers to common questions about STYRIVA, its AI-powered styling tools, clothing uploads, credits and privacy.

About STYRIVA

What is STYRIVA?

STYRIVA is an AI-powered styling assistant that helps you explore outfit combinations using clothing and accessory options you provide, together with details such as occasion, preferred vibe and weather.

What does STYRIVA actually do?

STYRIVA processes the information and images you submit to generate styling recommendations. It is designed to help you discover ways to combine pieces from your available wardrobe.

Do I need a large wardrobe to use STYRIVA?

No. You can start with the clothing and accessories available to you. STYRIVA is designed to help you explore combinations rather than requiring you to purchase an entirely new wardrobe.

Using STYRIVA

Do I need to create an account?

Some available features may be accessible without an account. Creating an account allows you to use account-based features where available. The app will indicate when registration or login is required.

What information do I provide?

Depending on the styling workflow, you may provide clothing or accessory images and details such as occasion, vibe and weather. The styling tool will show you which information is needed for that particular workflow.

Why does STYRIVA ask about the occasion?

An outfit suitable for a casual day may not be suitable for a formal event. The occasion gives the styling system additional context when evaluating possible combinations.

Why does weather matter?

Weather can affect comfort and practicality. Providing weather information helps the styling system consider those factors alongside the visual aspects of an outfit.

Can I use my own clothes?

Yes. STYRIVA is designed to work with the clothing and accessory images you provide, helping you explore combinations using pieces you already own.

AI Styling

How does the AI choose a look?

STYRIVA uses the information and images you provide to evaluate styling possibilities. Depending on the workflow, factors may include garment appearance, occasion, vibe, weather and overall compatibility. It then generates a styling recommendation.

Is the recommendation always correct?

No AI styling system can understand every personal preference or real-world situation perfectly. Recommendations are suggestions and may not always reflect your exact taste, fit requirements or needs. Use your own judgment when deciding what to wear.

Can I follow my own preferences instead?

Absolutely. STYRIVA is intended to assist your styling decisions, not replace your personal taste. You can adapt or ignore any recommendation to suit your preferences and comfort.

Does STYRIVA guarantee that generated images will look exactly like my clothes?

No. AI-generated results may differ from the original uploaded garments in appearance, colour, texture, proportions or other details. Always treat generated images as visual styling suggestions rather than exact representations of physical products.

Free Access and Credits

Is STYRIVA free to use?

STYRIVA may provide free access or complimentary usage allowances for eligible users and features. Additional usage may require credits where that option is offered. The available allowance and any applicable cost will be shown in the app.

What are STYRIVA credits?

Credits are usage units that may be required for certain AI-powered styling features. The number of credits required depends on the feature and the rules displayed in the app before you generate a result.

How many AI generations can I use?

Available generations may depend on your account status, complimentary usage allowance, available credits and the specific styling feature. Your current usage or available credits should be checked inside STYRIVA.

What happens when my free allowance is used?

When an applicable free allowance has been exhausted, the app may ask you to wait for another allowance, sign in, or use credits, depending on the feature and the rules currently available.

Do credits expire?

Any expiry conditions or other restrictions applicable to credits will be displayed with the relevant credit offer or purchase information. Please review those conditions before purchasing or using credits.

Are credit purchases subscriptions?

A one-time credit purchase and a recurring subscription are different payment arrangements. Check the relevant offer and checkout details to confirm the payment type before completing a transaction.

Usage allowances, credit requirements and available payment options may change as STYRIVA develops. Always refer to the current information displayed inside the app and at checkout.

Images and Privacy

What happens to the images I upload?

Uploaded images may be processed to provide the requested styling or image-generation features. Images may be handled by service providers involved in operating those features. For more information, please read our Privacy Policy.

Does STYRIVA require my email address?

Guest access may not require an email address. Account registration may require the information requested during signup. The information required depends on the feature you are using.

Should I upload someone else's photo?

Only upload images that you have the right or permission to use. Do not upload images of another person without appropriate permission, and avoid submitting private or sensitive images unless necessary for the feature.

Can I request deletion of my information?

You can contact STYRIVA to ask about deletion of account information or uploaded content. Requests may be subject to applicable legal requirements and the technical capabilities of the services involved in processing your information.

Where can I read about my privacy rights?

Please review our Privacy Policy for information about data collection, processing, storage, third-party services and applicable rights.
