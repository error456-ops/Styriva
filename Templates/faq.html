Frequently Asked Questions

Questions, answered.

Find answers to common questions about STYRIVA and its AI-powered styling service.

About STYRIVA

What is STYRIVA?

STYRIVA is an AI-powered styling tool that helps you create outfit recommendations using the clothing and accessory options you provide, along with information such as your occasion, preferred vibe, and weather.

What does STYRIVA actually do?

STYRIVA analyzes the options you provide and generates a curated styling recommendation. The goal is to help you decide how your available pieces can work together for a particular situation.

Do I need a large wardrobe to use STYRIVA?

No. STYRIVA is designed to work with the clothing and accessory choices you provide. You can use it to explore combinations from pieces you already own.

Using STYRIVA

Do I need to create an account?

No. STYRIVA currently does not require an account, signup, or login. You can use the available styling features directly without creating a user profile.

What information do I provide?

Depending on the styling workflow, you provide clothing or accessory images and information such as the occasion, vibe, and weather. The available options shown in the styling tool determine exactly what you can submit.

Why does STYRIVA ask about the occasion?

An outfit that works for a casual day may not be suitable for a formal event. The occasion gives the styling system context when evaluating the available combinations.

Why does weather matter?

Weather can affect both comfort and practicality. Providing weather information allows it to be considered alongside the visual aspects of the styling recommendation.

Can I use my own clothes?

Yes. STYRIVA is designed around the clothing and accessory choices you provide rather than requiring you to shop for an entirely new outfit.

AI styling

How does the AI choose a look?

STYRIVA considers the information you provide and evaluates the available styling options according to factors such as the occasion, vibe, weather, and overall compatibility. It then produces a curated recommendation.

Is the recommendation always correct?

No AI styling system can understand every personal preference or real-world situation perfectly. STYRIVA's recommendations are suggestions, and you should use your own judgment when deciding what to wear.

Can I follow my own preferences instead?

Absolutely. STYRIVA is intended as a styling assistant, not a replacement for personal taste. You can use its recommendations as a starting point and adapt them to your own preferences and comfort.

Free access and usage

Is STYRIVA currently free?

Yes. STYRIVA is currently available as a free service. There is no active premium subscription or payment requirement for the current service.

How many AI generations can I use each day?

The current STYRIVA service allows up to 2 AI generations per day.

What happens after I use both daily generations?

Once you have used your 2 daily AI generations, you will need to come back the next day when your daily allowance becomes available again.

Why is there a daily limit?

AI-powered processing requires computing resources. A daily usage limit helps STYRIVA manage those resources and keep the service available to users.

STYRIVA's current model is free-only. There is no active premium subscription, upgrade requirement, or payment requirement for using the current service.

Images and privacy

What happens to the images I upload?

Uploaded images may be processed as part of providing the styling service. For details about how information and uploaded content are handled, please review the STYRIVA Privacy Policy .

Does STYRIVA require my email address?

No. STYRIVA currently does not require an email address to use the styling service because an account is not required.

If you voluntarily contact STYRIVA through a support or contact channel that requests an email address, that information may be processed so that we can respond to your request.

Should I upload someone else's photo?

You should only upload images that you have permission to use and that are appropriate for the service.

Where can I read about my privacy rights?

Detailed information about data collection, processing, storage, and applicable user rights is available in our Privacy Policy .
