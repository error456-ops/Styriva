About STYRIVA

Personal styling,
made simpler.

STYRIVA is an AI-powered styling tool designed to help you make everyday outfit decisions using the clothing choices, occasion, vibe, and weather you provide.

What is STYRIVA?

Deciding what to wear can sometimes be surprisingly difficult. You may have several pieces that you like but still be unsure about which ones work best together or what would be appropriate for a particular occasion.

STYRIVA was created to make that decision easier. It is an AI-powered styling assistant that takes the options you provide and generates a personalized styling recommendation based on your selected occasion, vibe, and weather.

Instead of asking you to build an entirely new wardrobe, STYRIVA focuses on helping you make better use of the clothing and accessories you already have.

Why we built STYRIVA

Styling advice is often presented as a collection of general fashion rules. But what works for one person, occasion, or weather condition may not work for another.

STYRIVA was designed around a simpler idea: styling should consider the choices that are actually available to you.

By combining the information you provide with AI-based analysis, STYRIVA aims to turn a collection of clothing options into a practical look recommendation.

What STYRIVA considers

Your clothing choices

STYRIVA works with the clothing and accessory options you provide rather than assuming an unlimited wardrobe.

The occasion

The intended occasion helps determine whether a combination is appropriate for the situation.

Your chosen vibe

Your selected visual vibe helps guide the overall direction of the recommended look.

Weather

Weather information helps STYRIVA take practical conditions into account when creating a recommendation.

How STYRIVA works

Creating a styling recommendation is designed to be a simple process.

1

Provide your options

Upload the clothing and accessory choices you want STYRIVA to consider.

2

Tell STYRIVA about the occasion

Select the occasion, visual vibe, and relevant weather information.

3

AI analyzes the choices

4

Receive a styling recommendation

The result presents a curated look and styling guidance based on your selected options.

Our approach to AI styling

STYRIVA uses artificial intelligence to assist with styling decisions. The system considers the information and images provided by the user and generates a recommendation from the available choices.

The goal is not to replace personal taste. Instead, STYRIVA is designed to give you another perspective when you are deciding what to wear.

AI-generated styling recommendations are suggestions and may not always be suitable for every individual preference, situation, or condition. You remain responsible for deciding whether a recommended look is appropriate for you.

STYRIVA may process information and images that you provide while using the styling service. We believe users should be able to understand how their information is handled.
