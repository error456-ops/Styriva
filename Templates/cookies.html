Cookie Policy

This page explains how STYRIVA may use cookies and similar technologies when you visit or use our website.

Last updated: November 29, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device when you visit them. They can help websites remember information, maintain sessions, support security and provide certain website features.

Cookies may be temporary, meaning they are removed when you close your browser, or persistent, meaning they remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them.

2. How STYRIVA May Use Cookies

Depending on the features and technologies implemented on the website, STYRIVA may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Supporting essential website functionality.
  • Maintaining login sessions where applicable.
  • Protecting forms and requests against certain security threats.
  • Remembering preferences where applicable.
  • Understanding website performance if analytics are enabled.
  • Supporting advertising measurement if advertising technologies are implemented.

The specific cookies used may depend on your interactions with STYRIVA, your account status, browser and the technologies enabled on the website.

3. Essential and Security Cookies

Certain cookies may be necessary for STYRIVA to operate correctly and securely.

For example, Django-based websites may use cookies such as a session cookie to maintain a user's session and a CSRF cookie to help protect forms and requests against cross-site request forgery.

Depending on the website configuration, these cookies may support:

  • Maintaining a signed-in session.
  • Supporting secure form submissions.
  • Protecting certain website interactions.
  • Maintaining necessary website functionality.

Blocking essential cookies may prevent some parts of STYRIVA from functioning correctly.

4. Account and Session Cookies

When you register, sign in or use account-based features, STYRIVA may use session-related technologies to maintain your authenticated session.

These technologies may help the website recognise that you are signed in while you navigate between pages.

Session information is handled according to the website's configuration and applicable security practices.

5. Analytics Technologies

If analytics tools are enabled on STYRIVA, cookies or similar technologies may be used to understand how visitors interact with the website.

Depending on the analytics service, this may include information such as:

  • Pages visited and general navigation patterns.
  • Browser and device information.
  • Website performance and technical errors.
  • Interactions with website features.

This information may help us understand website usage and improve the functionality and user experience of STYRIVA.

Mentioning analytics in this policy does not mean that an analytics service is currently installed or active. Actual use depends on the technologies implemented on STYRIVA.

6. Advertising Technologies

If STYRIVA introduces advertising features, third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalise advertisements, subject to their own policies and applicable requirements.

For example, advertising services such as Google AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies when implemented.

Advertising technologies are not assumed to be active simply because they are mentioned in this policy.

7. Third-Party Services

STYRIVA uses external service providers to support certain platform functions, including AI processing, image services and hosting infrastructure.

Depending on how a particular service is integrated, third-party technologies may process information or use their own cookies or similar technologies.

Third-party providers operate under their own privacy and data-handling policies. Their use of cookies may be governed by their respective policies and settings.

Not every external service used by STYRIVA necessarily places cookies in your browser.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies Are Different

STYRIVA may also use certain browser technologies that are not technically cookies.

For example, a Progressive Web App (PWA) may use a service worker to support website caching and certain app-like experiences.

Browser storage mechanisms may also be used where implemented to support website functionality.

These technologies are different from traditional cookies, although they may perform related functions.

9. Managing Cookies

Most modern browsers allow you to manage cookies through their privacy or security settings.

Depending on your browser, you may be able to:

  • View cookies stored on your device.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Block some or all cookies.
  • Configure notifications about cookies.

Please remember that disabling certain cookies may affect login sessions, security features and other parts of STYRIVA.

Clearing browser storage or website data may also affect saved preferences and PWA functionality.

10. Cookie Consent and Preferences

Where consent is required by applicable law for non-essential cookies or similar technologies, STYRIVA should obtain the required consent before using those technologies.

Any available consent or preference controls should explain the relevant choices and how those choices can be changed.

Browser settings may also allow you to control certain cookies independently.

11. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when STYRIVA's website, technologies, services or applicable legal requirements change.

Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy or how cookies and similar technologies are used on STYRIVA, please visit our Contact page.

You can also review our Privacy Policy for more information about how personal information is handled.
