Cookie Policy

This page explains how STYRIVA may use cookies and similar technologies when you visit or use our website.

Last updated: August 11, 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that websites can store on your device when you visit them. They can help a website remember information, maintain necessary sessions, understand how the service is used, and support certain website features.

2. How STYRIVA may use cookies

STYRIVA may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Supporting essential website functionality.
  • Maintaining necessary website sessions.
  • Remembering certain preferences where applicable.
  • Understanding website usage and performance where applicable.
  • Supporting advertising and measurement technologies when applicable.

STYRIVA does not currently use cookies to maintain user login accounts because an account or login is not required to use the styling service.

3. Essential cookies

Some cookies may be necessary for the website to function correctly. For example, Django may use cookies to maintain necessary sessions and help protect forms and requests.

These cookies may be used for technical and security purposes rather than for maintaining a user account.

Disabling essential cookies may cause some parts of STYRIVA to stop working correctly.

4. Analytics cookies

If analytics services are enabled on STYRIVA, cookies or similar technologies may be used to understand information such as how visitors navigate the website, which pages are used, and whether technical problems occur.

Analytics information can help us improve the functionality and usability of STYRIVA.

5. Advertising cookies

STYRIVA may use third-party advertising services, such as Google AdSense, if advertising is enabled on the website.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, and personalize advertisements in accordance with their own policies and applicable requirements.

Advertising is not assumed to be active simply because this Cookie Policy mentions it. Advertising technologies will only be used if they are actually implemented on STYRIVA.

6. Third-party services

Some services used by STYRIVA may place or access cookies or similar technologies. The specific services used may change as the website develops.

Third-party services operate according to their own privacy and cookie policies.

7. Managing cookies

Most modern web browsers allow you to control or delete cookies through their settings.

You can generally choose to block cookies, delete existing cookies, or receive a notification before a cookie is stored. However, blocking essential cookies may affect the functionality of STYRIVA.

8. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when our website, technologies, services, or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy or how cookies are used on STYRIVA, please visit our Contact page.

You can also review our Privacy Policy for more information about how information is handled.
