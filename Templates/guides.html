STYRIVA Style Guides

Make better styling decisions.

Practical guides to help you understand outfit combinations, colors, occasions, weather, accessories, and everyday styling.

Good styling doesn't have to mean following complicated rules. These guides focus on simple principles you can use with the clothes you already own.

GUIDE 01

How to Choose an Outfit for an Occasion

The right outfit starts with understanding where you're going and what the situation calls for.

  • Consider how formal the occasion is.
  • Think about the setting and time of day.
  • Choose pieces that make you feel comfortable.
  • Keep the overall look appropriate rather than overly complicated.
GUIDE 02

Understanding Color Harmony

Color is one of the easiest ways to change the feeling of an outfit.

  • Start with a dominant color.
  • Add neutral pieces when several colors compete.
  • Use contrast deliberately.
  • Consider whether the colors create the mood you want.
GUIDE 03

Dressing for Different Weather

A stylish outfit also needs to work in the conditions you'll actually experience.

  • Consider temperature before appearance.
  • Use layers when conditions may change.
  • Choose suitable fabrics for the weather.
  • Think about practical accessories when necessary.
GUIDE 04

How to Balance an Outfit

Outfit balance is about how different pieces work together visually.

  • Consider proportions between loose and fitted pieces.
  • Avoid making every item compete for attention.
  • Use one or two visual focal points.
  • Keep the overall silhouette intentional.
GUIDE 05

Choosing Accessories

Accessories can complete an outfit, but the right amount depends on the rest of the look.

  • Consider the colors already present.
  • Match accessories to the formality of the outfit.
  • Use accessories to support rather than overwhelm the look.
  • Choose pieces that are practical for the occasion.
GUIDE 06

Styling the Clothes You Already Own

You don't always need something new. Small changes can create noticeably different combinations.

  • Start by identifying your most versatile pieces.
  • Try changing one element at a time.
  • Experiment with different combinations.
  • Build around pieces you already feel comfortable wearing.

A simple rule for everyday styling

Start with the occasion, then consider comfort and weather. From there, choose a main piece and build around it using compatible colors, proportions, and accessories.

The goal isn't to follow every fashion rule. The goal is to create a combination that makes sense for the situation and feels like you.
